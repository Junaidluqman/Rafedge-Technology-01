About Rafedge

A cybersecurity initiative built on practical learning

Rafedge Technology helps individuals and organizations understand cybersecurity risks, identify weaknesses through authorized testing, and improve their security practices. We also promote cybersecurity awareness and practical learning among aspiring security professionals.

Our goal is to provide cybersecurity services at low prices, so small businesses and SMEs can affordably test and secure their digital assets. We also aim to offer affordable cybersecurity skills training so young people can connect with us, learn, and earn.

Our long-term vision is to grow into a trusted cybersecurity service provider and technology organization in Pakistan.

Mission

To make cybersecurity affordable and accessible: low-cost security testing for small businesses and SMEs, and practical skills training for young people.

Vision

To become a trusted cybersecurity service provider and technology organization in Pakistan.

Services

Security services focused on clarity and responsibility

Practical assessments and guidance, delivered with proper authorization and an agreed scope.

Web Application Security Testing

Authorized testing of websites and web apps to uncover common weaknesses before attackers do.

  • OWASP Top 10 focused review
  • Authentication & session checks
  • Input handling & access control

Network Security Assessments

Review of network exposure to identify open services, misconfigurations, and risky entry points.

  • Host & service discovery
  • Exposure and configuration review
  • Prioritized recommendations

Vulnerability Assessment

Systematic identification and ranking of weaknesses so you know what to fix first.

  • Scanning & manual validation
  • Risk-based prioritization
  • Clear written reporting

Security Consulting

Guidance on improving everyday security practices for small teams and growing organizations.

  • Security best-practice guidance
  • Basic hardening recommendations
  • Awareness of common threats

Cybersecurity Awareness Training

Accessible sessions that help people recognize phishing, weak passwords, and other common risks.

  • Phishing & social engineering basics
  • Password & account hygiene
  • Safe online habits
